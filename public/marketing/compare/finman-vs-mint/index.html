The Key Differences

🤖

AI-First Architecture

Finman is built from the ground up with AI at the center. The AI CFO chat, auto-categorization, receipt scanning, and forecasting all use modern large language models and computer vision — not the rule-based automation that defined Mint's era.

🔒

No Ad-Based Model

Mint's business model was built on advertising and lead generation — serving you ads for credit cards and financial products. Finman earns revenue through subscriptions only. Your data is never sold or used for advertising.

👨‍👩‍👧

True Multi-User Support

Mint was built for single users. Finman's family finance feature supports multi-member households with shared budgets, split expenses, and privacy controls — a major gap Mint never addressed.

🔮

Forward-Looking Intelligence

Mint excelled at showing you where your money went. Finman adds where your money is going — with 3, 6, and 12-month cash flow forecasts that account for your recurring expenses, seasonal patterns, and planned events.

Who Should Choose Finman?

✓ Choose Finman if you:

  • Want a conversational AI CFO to advise you
  • Need receipt scanning to track cash expenses
  • Share finances with a partner or family
  • Want proactive forecasts, not just spending history
  • Care about data privacy and no advertising
  • Were a Mint user looking for a modern alternative
  • Want native Android and iOS apps

→ Consider alternatives if you:

  • Primarily need credit monitoring (Credit Karma is better)
  • Want a completely free product with no premium tier
  • Need direct bank connection for account management
  • Only need basic spending tracking with no AI features
