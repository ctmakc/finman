FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you start.

What is Finman?
Finman is an AI-powered personal finance manager that works as your always-on CFO. It tracks your income, expenses, budgets, investments, and net worth — and uses AI to give you insights, forecasts, and actionable advice in plain English.
Does Finman work on Android and iOS?
Yes. Finman has native Android and iOS apps built with Capacitor, giving you full access to all features on mobile — including receipt scanning via your phone camera, offline support, and biometric login.
How does the AI CFO feature work?
The AI CFO is a conversational chat interface powered by Anthropic Claude, OpenAI GPT-4, or Google Gemini (your choice). Ask it anything about your finances — spending patterns, budget advice, debt payoff strategies — and it answers using your real financial data.
Can Finman scan receipts automatically?
Yes. Finman uses vision AI to extract merchant name, amount, date, and category from a photo of any receipt. Just point your camera and tap. The extracted data auto-creates a transaction — no typing required.
Is Finman suitable for families?
Absolutely. Finman supports multi-member households with shared accounts, split expenses, family budgets, and individual spending views all in one dashboard. Each member has private spending areas while household totals stay visible to all.
How is Finman different from Mint or YNAB?
Unlike legacy apps, Finman is built AI-first. Every feature — from categorization to forecasting to debt payoff — is powered by machine learning. You get proactive insights and a conversational AI advisor, not just a spreadsheet on your phone. Compare Finman vs Mint →
