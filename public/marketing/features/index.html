Smart Automation

Auto-Categorize Transactions

Manual transaction tagging is a chore that kills financial tracking habits. Finman uses machine learning to read your transaction descriptions and assign accurate spending categories automatically — before you even open the app.

The model is pre-trained on thousands of merchant names and improves with every correction you make. Over time, it learns your specific spending patterns and becomes more accurate than any rule-based system.

  • 50+ default categories with sub-categories
  • Custom category creation and rules
  • Split transactions across multiple categories
  • Learns from your manual corrections

Vision AI

Receipt Scanning

Stop losing paper receipts and stop typing expense details manually. Finman's vision AI extracts merchant name, total amount, date, and itemized line items from any receipt photo — crumpled or faded receipts included.

The extracted data automatically creates a transaction and links the receipt image. Everything is searchable. Tax time becomes a matter of filtering your receipt archive rather than digging through shoeboxes.

  • Works on any smartphone camera (Android + iOS)
  • Extracts merchant, amount, date, line items
  • Receipt image stored and linked to transaction
  • Batch scanning for expense reports

  • Monthly, weekly, or custom period budgets
  • Per-category and total household budgets
  • Proactive overspend alerts
  • AI-suggested budget amounts

Financial Health

Net Worth Dashboard

Your net worth — assets minus liabilities — is the single most important number in personal finance. Finman calculates it automatically from your connected accounts and tracks it over time with daily precision.

Watch it grow as you pay down debt and build savings. The historical chart reveals patterns: which months drag your net worth down and which events (bonuses, car purchases, investment gains) drive it up.

  • Real-time asset and liability tracking
  • Daily net worth snapshots
  • Historical trend chart
  • Net worth breakdown by category
