Sample statements for the Finman Statement Agent

Download a file and upload it in Finman: More → Banks & imports → Import a statement. The agent reads the PDF or photo, then Finman checks that the opening balance plus every row equals the closing balance, to the cent. Nothing is written until that check passes and you press Import.

  1. Sign in at this sandbox with the judge account from the submission form.
  2. Pick the account in the statement's currency (CAD, USD, AED or EUR are already there).
  3. Read the balance proof line, then press Import, or see where the gap is.
