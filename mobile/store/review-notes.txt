Finman is a personal finance app (budgets, statement import, goals, debts, net worth, AI assistant). Every feature is free; there are no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads in the app.

SIGN IN
Demo account: DEMO_EMAIL (password in the demo account fields). On the first screen tap "Sign in", enter the email and password. The account already has sample accounts, transactions, budgets, goals and debts.
You can also create a new account with any email, or use "Continue with Google" / "Sign in with Apple". A new account starts empty and offers "Explore with demo data".

STATEMENT IMPORT
Dashboard or Transactions -> Import CSV. Pick a CSV, OFX or QFX bank statement file; Finman shows a preview of the rows and columns, and nothing is saved until you confirm. The app never asks for bank passwords and does not connect to banks.

AI CFO
Menu -> AI CFO. AI features are off until the user turns them on (opt-in prompt). Type a question, e.g. "How much can I save this month?". Answers use only the user's own data. The chat is private; users cannot message each other.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Menu -> Settings -> Danger zone -> Delete account. Type DELETE (and the password for email accounts) and confirm; the account and all its data are erased. Web alternative: https://finman.money/delete-account/

OTHER
External links (website, privacy policy, Google sign-in) open in the system browser.
